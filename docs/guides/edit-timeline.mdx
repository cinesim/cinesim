This page assumes you have assets in the Media Pool. For the model behind any of it, see
[The timeline](../concepts/timeline.mdx).

## Build a sequence from assets

Select assets in the Media Pool in the order you want them, then create a timeline from the
selection. Cinesim places them end to end, creates the tracks it needs, and makes the new sequence
active — in one undo step.

Video files with sound produce linked video and audio clips on separate tracks. They stay in sync
through every subsequent edit.

## Choose a tool

| Key          | Tool      | Use it to              |
| ------------ | --------- | ---------------------- |
| <kbd>A</kbd> | Selection | Move clips, drag edges |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Trim      | Adjust an edit point   |
| <kbd>B</kbd> | Blade     | Cut at the pointer     |

Press <kbd>S</kbd> to toggle snapping. While you drag, Cinesim previews the result and shows collision
feedback when a placement would overlap; the edit commits when you release.

## Trim, split, and delete

Drag a clip's edge to trim it. Trimming clamps any existing fades to the new duration.

Split the selected clip at the playhead with <kbd>⌘&#92;</kbd>, or cut anywhere with the Blade tool. A
split preserves the outer fades and clears the two new interior edges.

Delete the selection with <kbd>⌫</kbd>. On linked clips, every one of these applies to the pair as a
single edit.

## Shape fades

Drag a clip's fade handle to set a linear fade-in or fade-out — opacity on video, gain on audio.
Combined fades cannot exceed the clip's duration, and the drag commits as one undo step.

## Manage tracks

Add video, overlay, or audio tracks and rename, mute, lock, or reorder them. Index `0` is the
uppermost track, and upper visual tracks composite over lower ones.

To remove a track, first unlock it and clear its clips — Cinesim refuses to remove a track that
still holds anything.

Adjust track height and timeline zoom to taste; neither is a project edit, so neither enters undo
history.

## Play back your cut

| Key                                    | Action                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>                       | Play or pause           |
| <kbd>J</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd> | Reverse, pause, forward |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>              | Previous or next frame  |
| <kbd>Home</kbd>                        | Go to the beginning     |

The viewer offers fit, 50%, 100%, and 200% display scales, configurable grids and safe-area guides,
and fullscreen. Its header reports whether you are watching **Proxy** or **Original** media.

Hovering an asset in the Media Pool previews that source in the viewer and restores your timeline
frame when you leave — timeline transport and source preview stay separate.

## Undo

Press <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> to reverse one completed gesture, not a fragment of one. Panel resizes, selection, zoom,
and track height are interface state and never appear in history.
