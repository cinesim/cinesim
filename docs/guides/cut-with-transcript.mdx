---
title: Cut from the transcript
description: Transcribe the active timeline, navigate by speech, and make exact ripple edits as text.
---

The **Cut** workspace turns the active timeline into an editable document without creating a second
timeline. Cut and Edit share the same sequence, clips, playhead, playback runtime, command history,
and undo stack. Switch among **Media**, **Cut**, and **Edit** with <kbd>⌘1</kbd>, <kbd>⌘2</kbd>, and

<kbd>⌘3</kbd> (use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> on Windows and Linux).

## Generate a transcript

Open a timeline, choose **Cut**, and select **Transcribe timeline**. You can also generate or retry an
individual asset transcript from its Media Pool card. Every signed-in user can use transcription
when the Cinesim service has configured its OpenRouter gateway; there is currently no billing or
plan gate.

Before the first transcription for an account, Cinesim explains that the operation is remote and
asks for consent. Bounded audio chunks go through OpenRouter to Deepgram Nova-3. The API credential
never enters the desktop app. The generated word timings, speaker clusters, utterances, paragraphs,
punctuation, formatting, detected language, confidence, and retained filler words are stored under
`.video/transcripts/`, so they can be discarded and regenerated without changing the project.

Media cards show **Not transcribed**, **Transcript queued**, **Transcribing**, **Transcript ready**,
or **Transcript failed**. Queued or running work can be canceled, and a failed or canceled transcript
can be retried. Cinesim does not invent word timings if
the provider omits them, because inaccurate timing could delete the wrong media.

## Navigate the cut

Click a word to seek to its exact timeline interval. Drag across words or Shift-click to extend the
selection. Speaker chips open speaker actions, while the header can search the transcript or filter
to one generated speaker. Overlapping speakers remain visibly overlapping instead of being
reordered into false prose.

Long pauses appear as filled silence pills with their exact duration. Empty timeline gaps use a
separate hollow treatment. Missing, queued, running, and failed transcript coverage stays visible,
so the document never suggests that an untranscribed interval contains no content.

## Edit as text

With words, silence, or a timeline gap selected:

- choose **Play** to audition the exact range and stop at its end;
- press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or choose **Ripple delete**, to remove the exact
  selected time and close the sequence-wide gap;
- choose **Lift** to remove the selected time without closing the gap.

Each operation is one validated canonical command and one undo step, even when Cinesim must split a
linked video/audio pair at two boundaries and move later material on multiple tracks. A locked track
rejects the whole operation without a partial edit. Search fields and other text inputs keep their
normal Delete behavior.

## Use the adaptive timeline

Cut starts with a collapsed story row across the bottom. Dialogue clips appear in narrative order;
B-roll, music, and overlapping dialogue appear as compact `+B`, `+M`, and `+S` indicators. The exact
transcript selection is highlighted inside the corresponding story blocks.

Drag the divider upward for a compact time-based view and farther for the full multitrack editor.
The same responsive Timeline component is used in Edit, so resizing never changes project state,
selection, playhead, or history. Cut remembers its transcript/right-column, viewer/Media Pool, and
timeline sizes independently from Edit.

Generated speaker names and transcript text are derived in this first release. Durable speaker
renames, merges, and text corrections require a future versioned canonical annotation format; the
app does not hide that limitation by writing corrections into disposable transcript artifacts.
